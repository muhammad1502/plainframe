    What it removes

    • LocationGPS coordinates, altitude, direction, city and country
    • Camera and deviceMake, model, lens, serial numbers, software and dates
    • AI generation dataPrompts, seeds and settings from Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI, NovelAI and others
    • Content CredentialsC2PA manifests from Firefly, ChatGPT, Photoshop and cameras
    • Author and edit historyXMP and IPTC fields, keywords, captions and document IDs
    • Hidden imagesThumbnails, motion photo clips and data tucked after the image

    What it can't remove

    Watermarks hidden in the pixels themselves, such as Google SynthID, survive any metadata cleaner. So does anything visible in the picture. AI detectors that judge the pixels may still flag an image.

    Runs on this device. It makes no network requests.
